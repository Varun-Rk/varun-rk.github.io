# Varun Marthand — AI Engineer

**Fly through my work, all the way to the Sun.**

This is my portfolio, built as a comic-book space journey. You pilot a small ship from the
cockpit to the Sun. Along the way, every project I've built turns up as an encounter in
deep space, and each one opens a comic page about the work.

I'm an AI engineer in Pune, India. I build AI agents, computer vision and applied ML systems.

## How to fly

- **Scroll** to fly forward. Scroll back to rewind.
- **Click empty space** (or tap on a phone) to fire missiles. Shoot down the first meteor
  before it hits you.
- **Sound is on** from your first click, tap or key press (browsers don't allow it sooner).
  Every encounter has its own sound effects. Use **Mute** in the top bar to turn it off.
- **Jump anywhere** with the dots on the right, or go straight to the end with "Go to the Sun".
- **Move the mouse** to steer the ship a little.

## The route

1. **The cockpit** — say hello to the pilot.
2. **Pilot's log** — who I am, how I work, and my GitHub activity as "flight hours".
3. **Eight missions** — one project each (see below).
4. **Ports of call** — a star chart of the places I've worked.
5. **Armory** — the tools I use, racked up like weapons.
6. **The Sun** — the end of the line, and how to reach me.

Six chapter cards crawl into the distance between the acts, from "The pilot" to "The Sun".

## The missions

The missions come in three acts. First the agents, which answer, act on their own, then learn
whether their advice worked. Then the data and ML products underneath. Last, the computer vision
work where I learned to make models work on the factory floor.

| # | Mission | Encounter | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Conversational AI Agent for Marketing Analytics | A meteor | ~90 tools across 13 skills, loaded only when a question needs them |
| 02 | Autonomous Ad-Account Agents | A dying star | 9 agents that never wait to be asked |
| 03 | AI Inbox & Outcome Tracking | A space station | Daily checks on whether each recommendation actually worked |
| 04 | Bayesian Marketing-Mix Modeling | A docking probe | Priors tuned automatically from real platform data, with no hand-fitting |
| 05 | Multilingual Content Categorization | A distant signal | ~10M page visits a day, each routed to a translation model picked for its language |
| 06 | Platform Backend & Tracking SDKs | A phone satellite | Ad-platform ingestion, Django APIs, and mobile SDKs that start tracking in 2 lines |
| 07 | Video Analytics for Automotive Assembly Lines | A scanning drone | −50% per-frame latency after moving inference to TensorRT |
| 08 | Process Monitoring for Chemical Manufacturing | Three moons | Exact phase-change timing (liquid to powder), live at <100ms across three streams |

Each mission page covers the problem, what I built, the result, an animated diagram of how
it flows, and what's under the hood.

## For every visitor

- **Phones:** the whole journey works on a phone.
- **Reduced motion:** if your system asks for less motion, the cards fade in calmly and the
  shaking, flashes and flying rocks are switched off.
- **No JavaScript:** everything reads as a plain comic page.
- **Keyboard:** you can tab through every card and link, and there's a "Skip to contact"
  link at the top.

## About this site

Hand-built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no frameworks. All the art is drawn in
code: the ship, the planets, the Sun and every encounter. The sounds are synthesised live in
the browser, and the flight-hours graph is refreshed from GitHub every day.

To run it yourself, serve this folder with any static server, for example
`python3 -m http.server 8000`, and open <http://localhost:8000/>.
